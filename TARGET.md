## Quiz app (built 5 Nov 2026)
- File: অঙ্গনওয়াড়ি-প্রস্তুতি-App.html (opens in any browser, works offline). Also published online as a private Claude artifact.
- 471 questions, all with answers and Bengali "আরও জানুন" explanations (maths: step-by-step).
- Every answer was solved twice independently (extract pass + separate blind verification). Any question where the two disagreed, or that had two defensible answers / a missing correct option / needed a diagram, was dropped (~45 dropped).
- Paper answer keys were checked too; 7 printed answers were wrong and were corrected.
- Supervisor 2017 is actually a Kerala PSC paper: Kerala-only and outdated questions removed.
- Raw data: 3-App-Data/questions.json
